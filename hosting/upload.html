2The list

A .txt file in the usual format: day headings, then each event's title with its link on the next line. Lines under a link are notes for the reader, e.g. the place and time for an Instagram post.

Choose a .txt file or drop it here

Day headings can be in Russian, Hebrew or English, with or without a date ("Четверг 15.10"). The same link under several days becomes one event on all of them.
Each page is read and translated to English, Hebrew and Russian — about 15–30 seconds per event.
